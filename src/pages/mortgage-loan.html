When LAP makes sense

Large, long-term money at a sensible rate.

If you need ₹20 lakh for a business expansion, a medical treatment or your child's overseas education, an unsecured loan becomes expensive and short. Securing it against a property you already own can cut the rate and stretch repayment over years.

The trade-off is real: the property becomes collateral, and missing payments can put it at risk. We walk you through the LTV, the lender's valuation, the reset clause and the consequences of default before you sign anything.

  • Business expansion, machinery or working capital
  • Consolidating multiple high-cost loans into one EMI
  • Education, medical or wedding expenses requiring a larger amount

Who can apply

Eligibility and documents

Eligibility

  • Age 21 to 70 years at loan maturity
  • Owner of a residential, commercial or industrial property
  • Salaried or self-employed with documented income
  • CIBIL score of 700+ preferred
  • Clear and marketable title, with no litigation
  • Existing mortgages disclosed; LTV within lender limits

Documents

  • PAN, Aadhaar and address proof
  • Income proof — salary slips/ITR, or business financials and bank statements
  • Title deed and chain of ownership documents
  • Encumbrance certificate and property tax receipts
  • Approved plan and occupancy certificate
  • Existing loan statement, if the property is already financed

Before you apply

Mortgage loan FAQs

How much can I borrow against my property?

Most lenders fund up to 70% of the assessed market value for residential property, and 50–60% for commercial or industrial property. The final amount also depends on your income and existing obligations.

Can I continue living in or renting out the property?

Yes. A mortgage creates a charge on the title; it does not transfer possession. You can continue to live in the home or collect rent from tenants, subject to the loan agreement.

Can I get a loan against a jointly owned property?

Yes, with the consent and co-signature of all co-owners. Lenders will verify each owner's KYC and may require all owners to be co-applicants.

What if my property is under construction?

Construction-linked or incomplete properties are harder to mortgage, but some lenders accept them with an approved plan and progress certification. We will tell you which lenders are realistic for your property.

Is LAP better than a business loan?

LAP usually offers a larger amount, a lower rate and a longer tenure, but it puts your property at risk. An unsecured business loan is faster and safer for smaller, short-term needs. We compare both structures before recommending one.
