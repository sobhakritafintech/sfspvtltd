Who can apply

Eligibility and documents

Eligibility

  • Age 21 to 65 years
  • Salaried with 6+ months' employment, or self-employed with 2+ years' business
  • Minimum net monthly income of ₹15,000 (varies by lender and vehicle)
  • CIBIL score of 700+ preferred
  • A valid driving licence is recommended but not always mandatory for the applicant

Documents

  • PAN, Aadhaar and address proof
  • 3 months' salary slips and 6 months' bank statement (salaried)
  • 2 years' ITR and business bank statement (self-employed)
  • Proforma invoice / quotation from the dealer
  • Vehicle insurance and registration documents (post-purchase)
  • For used vehicles: RC, valuation report and NOC from the existing financier

Before you apply

Car loan FAQs

Can I get 100% on-road funding?

Some lenders offer up to 100% of the on-road price for strong profiles and select models, but most approvals cover 80–90%. A higher down payment usually means a lower EMI and a better rate.

Do you finance used cars?

Yes. Used car loans typically fund 70–90% of the vehicle's assessed value, with tenure and rate depending on the car's age, condition and ownership history. We help arrange the valuation and NOC.

Can I get a loan without a driving licence?

Some lenders allow the loan in the name of a non-driver if the vehicle is registered to and driven by a licensed family member. It is lender-specific, so we check before you apply.

What happens to the RC after the loan?

The lender's name is recorded as hypothecatee on the registration certificate. Once the loan is fully repaid, you receive an NOC and Form 35 to remove the hypothecation from the RC.

Can I prepay the car loan early?

Yes, usually after six EMI payments, with foreclosure charges as per your lender's policy. We help you calculate whether prepaying or investing the surplus makes more sense.
