1. Who we are

Sobhakrita Fintech Solutions Private Limited (“Sobhakrita”, “we”, “us”, “our”) is a private limited company incorporated in India, with its registered office at #20-90B/1, Naidu Buildings Area, Mittoor, Chittoor, Chittoor, Andhra Pradesh, 517001. Our CIN is U65990AP2024PTC000000.

We operate as a loan facilitator and Direct Selling Agent (DSA) for banks and RBI-regulated non-banking financial companies (NBFCs). We help individuals and businesses understand, compare and apply for credit products. We are not a lender and we do not sanction or disburse loans in our own name.

For the personal data you share with us directly, Sobhakrita acts as a Data Fiduciary under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (the “DPDP Act”). When a bank or NBFC processes your data for its own loan application, that lender acts as an independent Data Fiduciary and its own privacy notice applies.

2. Scope of this policy

This policy covers personal data we collect through:

  • this website, including enquiry, application, careers and book-a-call forms;
  • phone calls, WhatsApp messages, SMS and email exchanges with our team;
  • documents you share at our Chittoor office or through a lender portal;
  • referrals and field visits connected to a loan application; and
  • job applications submitted to us.

It does not cover the practices of banks, NBFCs, credit bureaus, payment providers or other third parties. Please read their privacy notices separately.

4. How we collect it

  • Directly from you — when you fill a form, call us, message us on WhatsApp, email documents or visit the office.
  • From your authorised representatives — a family member or colleague helping with your application, where you have permitted them.
  • From lenders and partners — status updates, sanction terms and queries relating to your file.
  • From credit information companies — where you have authorised a bureau enquiry as part of a lender application.
  • Automatically — limited technical data when you browse this website, as described in the cookies section.

5. Why we process it

We process personal data for the following purposes:

  • responding to your enquiry and providing the consultation you requested;
  • assessing your eligibility and preparing a comparative lender shortlist;
  • submitting your application to a lender you have agreed to, and following it through sanction and disbursal;
  • verifying documents and meeting our KYC, anti-money laundering and record-keeping obligations;
  • servicing your file after disbursal, including repayment coordination and grievance handling;
  • complying with applicable law, court orders and regulatory requests;
  • improving our services, provided any analysis uses aggregated or de-identified data where practicable; and
  • assessing job applications.
Consent and lawful basis. Where the DPDP Act requires consent, we ask for it separately and clearly — for example, before sharing your details with a lender or making a credit-bureau enquiry. You may withdraw consent at any time by writing to services@sobhakrita.com. Withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out, or processing permitted under a legitimate use recognised by the DPDP Act, such as complying with a legal obligation or responding to a medical emergency.

6. Who we share it with

We share personal data only where it is necessary for the purpose you have asked us to serve. Recipients may include:

  • Banks and RBI-regulated NBFCs whose loan products you have agreed to apply for;
  • Credit information companies such as TransUnion CIBIL, Experian, Equifax and CRIF High Mark, where you have authorised an enquiry;
  • Service providers who support us — for example, document verification agencies, field verification partners, communication platforms and IT hosting providers — under contracts that restrict their use of the data;
  • Professional advisers such as auditors and lawyers, where required;
  • Government authorities, regulators or courts where we are legally required to disclose; and
  • a successor entity in the event of a merger, restructuring or sale of the business, subject to equivalent protections.

We do not sell personal data. We do not share your data with unrelated marketers or lead brokers. If a partner introduces you to us, we may confirm the status of your enquiry to that partner in limited terms.

7. Credit bureaus and CIBIL

A loan application usually requires the lender to obtain your credit information report. This is a hard enquiry and may affect your credit score. We will tell you before such an enquiry is made and will not initiate one merely because you browsed this site, booked a consultation or used the EMI calculator.

You can obtain a free full credit report from each credit information company once a year, and you have the right to dispute inaccurate information directly with the bureau and the lender.

8. Cookies and analytics

This website is built as a static site. It does not set advertising cookies or run third-party behavioural tracking by default. It may use:

  • Essential storage required for basic functionality, such as remembering a navigation state during your visit;
  • Aggregated analytics, if enabled by the site operator, to understand which pages are visited and how the site performs. Such data is used in aggregate and is not used to identify you personally; and
  • Third-party embeds, such as the Google Maps frame on our Contact page, which may set cookies under the provider's own policy.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking essential storage may affect some site features. We do not use cookies to make automated decisions about your creditworthiness.

9. How long we keep data

We retain personal data only as long as it is needed for the purpose it was collected, or as required by law:

  • Enquiries that do not become applications — generally up to 12 months, then deleted or de-identified;
  • Loan files — for the period required by the lender, usually the loan tenure plus the statutory record-retention period applicable to financial records;
  • KYC records — as required under applicable anti-money laundering and KYC rules;
  • Grievance records — for the period needed to demonstrate fair resolution, generally up to 3 years;
  • Job applications — up to 12 months unless you ask us to delete them sooner or to keep them for future openings; and
  • Website technical logs — typically up to 90 days, unless needed to investigate a security incident.

10. How we protect data

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, including access controls, restricted sharing, encrypted channels for document transfer where available, and staff training on confidentiality. Paper files are kept in a locked office and digital files in access-controlled storage.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. If a personal data breach occurs, we will notify you and the Data Protection Board of India as required under the DPDP Act, and take steps to contain the incident.

11. Your rights as a Data Principal

Subject to the DPDP Act and applicable exceptions, you have the right to:

  • Access a summary of the personal data we process about you and the identity of the recipients with whom it has been shared;
  • Correct inaccurate or misleading data, and complete incomplete data;
  • Erase data that is no longer necessary for the purpose it was collected, unless retention is required by law;
  • Withdraw consent at any time, with effect from the date of withdrawal;
  • Nominate another individual to exercise your rights in the event of death or incapacity;
  • Grievance redressal — complain to our Grievance Officer, and if unresolved, to the Data Protection Board of India; and
  • Be free from discriminatory treatment for exercising your rights.

To exercise any of these rights, write to services@sobhakrita.com with your full name and registered mobile number. We may ask you to verify your identity before acting on a request. We aim to respond within 30 days, or sooner where required.

12. Children

Our services are intended for individuals aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. A loan application from a minor is not valid. If you believe a child has submitted personal data to us, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

13. Cross-border transfers

We primarily store data in India. Some service providers may process limited data outside India. Where that happens, we do so in accordance with the DPDP Act and any restriction notified by the Central Government, and we require comparable safeguards from the recipient.

14. Third-party links

This website links to lender websites, map services and social media platforms. We do not control their privacy practices. Once you leave our site or enter a lender's portal, that organisation's privacy policy governs the data you provide.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in law, our services or our data practices. The revised version will be posted on this page with a new “last updated” date. If a change materially affects how we use data you have already shared, we will notify you through a reasonable channel where required.
