Who can apply

Eligibility and documents

Indicative criteria for unsecured business loans; secured facilities can be more flexible.

Eligibility

  • Age 21 to 65 years
  • Business vintage of 2+ years (3+ for some lenders)
  • Annual turnover of ₹10 lakh or more, with filed ITRs
  • Profitable business with healthy banking transactions
  • Promoter CIBIL score of 700+ preferred

Documents

  • PAN and Aadhaar of proprietor, partners or directors
  • Business proof — GST certificate, shop licence or incorporation deed
  • Udyam registration certificate — we can help you apply for or correct it
  • e-PAN is accepted; download it from the Income Tax e-filing portal or NSDL
  • 2 years' ITR with computation and audited financials
  • 12 months' bank statement of the primary current account
  • GST returns for the last 12 months (where applicable)
  • Office/shop ownership proof or rent agreement

Before you apply

Business loan FAQs

Can I get a business loan without collateral?

Yes. Many NBFCs and banks offer unsecured business loans up to ₹50 lakh based on GST turnover, banking and credit history. Amounts above that, or weaker profiles, may need property or stock hypothecation.

My business is new. Can I still apply?

Most unsecured lenders need at least two years of vintage and filed ITRs. If your business is younger, a secured loan, a co-applicant with income, or a smaller ticket NBFC product may be possible. We will tell you honestly what is realistic.

Do you look at my personal credit score?

Yes. For proprietorships and small companies, the promoter's CIBIL score is a major input. A score of 700+ improves both approval odds and pricing.

How is the loan amount decided?

Lenders typically assess 10–30% of annual turnover, or a multiple of monthly banking credits, adjusted for existing obligations. We help you arrive at a figure your cash flow can service comfortably.

Can I get an overdraft instead of a term loan?

Yes, for eligible businesses. A cash credit or overdraft lets you draw funds as needed and pay interest only on the utilised amount — useful for inventory cycles. We compare both structures for you.

I don't have a Udyam certificate. Can you help?

Yes. Udyam registration is free on the government portal and many MSME lenders prefer it. We can walk you through the application, check that your business details and NIC codes are correct, and help you download the certificate. When a lender accepts e-PAN, we can use it in place of a physical PAN card — subject to that lender's KYC rules.

Do I need insurance with a business loan?

It depends on the lender and the security. Property-backed facilities usually require property insurance, and some lenders bundle loan-protection cover. We explain what is mandatory, what is optional, and help you compare the premium before you sign. Insurance is never a condition of our service.
