About Sobhakrita

Good finance begins with good advice.

Sobhakrita Fintech Solutions Private Limited is a Chittoor-based loan facilitator built around one idea — that every family and business deserves to understand the loan they are signing, and to get a fair deal on it.

3+ Years of combined team experience
Our story

Why is borrowing still so hard to understand?

That question started Sobhakrita. In Chittoor and across Andhra Pradesh, we met families who had been approved for a loan they could not comfortably repay, and businesses that had been rejected by one lender without ever knowing another would have said yes.

So we built the opposite of a sales counter. We sit on your side of the table, read the fine print with you, compare what different banks and NBFCs are actually offering, and tell you when a smaller loan — or no loan at all — is the wiser choice.

As a Direct Selling Agent for leading banks and RBI-regulated NBFCs, we earn only when your loan is sanctioned. That keeps our incentives aligned with one thing: getting you a genuine approval on terms you can live with.

How we work

Principles we do not negotiate on

We do not sell loans

We match requirements to lenders. If your file fits three banks, we show you three — with the real rate and the real catch, if any.

Fees in writing

No application, consultation, file or advance fee in our own name. Our minimal service fee applies only after disbursal, with your written consent, and every charge is disclosed in writing before documentation begins. Lender charges are shown in the Key Fact Statement.

Everything in writing

You receive a checklist, a comparison summary and a copy of the lender's sanction terms before you sign.

Support after disbursal

Repayment dates, NACH mandates, part-prepayment, foreclosure and credit-report corrections — we stay reachable.

Udyam, e-PAN & insurance

We help business owners obtain or correct their Udyam certificate, accept e-PAN for KYC where the lender allows it, and help you compare property and loan-protection insurance.
