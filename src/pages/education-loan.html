Who can apply

Eligibility and documents

Eligibility

  • Indian citizen aged 18 years or above
  • Confirmed admission to a recognised institution and course
  • A co-applicant (parent, guardian or spouse) with documented income
  • Co-applicant CIBIL score of 700+ preferred
  • Collateral for loans above the lender's unsecured threshold (often ₹7.5 lakh)
  • Valid passport and visa for overseas study

Documents

  • Admission letter, fee structure and course duration proof
  • Mark sheets and certificates of previous qualifications
  • PAN, Aadhaar, photograph and address proof of student and co-applicant
  • Co-applicant income proof — salary slips, ITR or business financials
  • Collateral documents, if the loan is secured
  • Passport, visa and university I-20/offer letter for overseas courses

Before you apply

Education loan FAQs

Can I get an education loan without collateral?

Many lenders offer unsecured education loans up to ₹7.5 lakh, and some go higher for premier institutions. Above that, collateral or a third-party guarantee is usually required. We match the amount to lenders whose thresholds fit your file.

When does repayment start?

Typically after the course ends, plus a grace or moratorium period of up to 12 months. Interest continues to accrue during study; some lenders allow simple interest servicing to reduce the final burden.

Is an overseas education loan harder to get?

It involves more documentation — passport, visa, university offer, cost of attendance — and usually higher amounts. Lenders with approved-institution lists have different criteria; we check eligibility before you commit to a deposit.

Can my parents be co-applicants?

Yes, and it is usually required. A parent or guardian's income and credit history are assessed alongside the student's profile. A strong co-applicant can also reduce the rate or remove the need for collateral.

Does the loan cover living expenses?

Most lenders cover tuition, hostel, books, equipment and examination fees, and many include living expenses for overseas courses within an overall cap. The exact components are listed in the sanction letter.
