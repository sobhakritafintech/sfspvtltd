Questions, answered

Before you buy cover

Do I need insurance along with my loan?

Not always, and never as a condition we invent. Some lenders require property or borrower cover for certain secured loans, and it is usually wise to protect a home loan with term cover. We explain what the lender requires and what is optional, so you can decide with full information.

How much cover do I actually need?

A common starting point for term cover is 10–15 times your annual income, plus outstanding loans, minus existing cover and liquid savings. Health cover is usually sized to family size and city hospital costs. We work these numbers with you instead of selling a fixed plan.

Which insurers do you work with?

Our life insurance partner is IndiaFirst Life. For health, motor and business cover we compare plans from leading insurers and their authorised channels. Availability varies by product, location and underwriting policy.

Will you help if I need to claim?

Yes. Call or WhatsApp us and we will help you gather documents, raise the intimation with the insurer and follow up until the claim is settled. Claim decisions rest with the insurer, but you will not be left to navigate the process alone.
