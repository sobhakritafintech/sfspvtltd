Common questions

Before you book

Is the consultation really free?

Yes — the call, eligibility check and lender comparison are free, with no obligation to apply. There is no application, consultation or file fee. we charge a minimal service fee only after loan disbursal, with your written consent. Every fee is explained in writing before documentation begins. See fees & charges.

What should I keep ready?

Nothing formal for the first call. If you have them, keep your latest salary slip or ITR, a bank statement and your CIBIL report handy. For a property loan, the property's approximate value and location help us give a realistic answer.

Will you run a credit check on the call?

No. We do not run a bureau enquiry just because you booked a consultation. A hard enquiry happens only when you agree to a formal application with a specific lender.

Can I book for a family member?

Yes, if they know you are sharing their number. Please use their name and mobile number in the form, and mention in the notes that you are helping them. We will speak to the applicant directly before collecting any personal documents.

Do you visit homes or businesses?

For home loans, mortgages, business loans and industrial loans, a doorstep or site visit is often part of the lender's process. We can arrange it after the initial call, within Chittoor district and nearby areas.
