Who can apply

Eligibility and documents

Indicative criteria. Small crop loans are assessed differently from tractor or large term loans.

Eligibility

  • Age 18 to 70 years, subject to lender policy
  • Owner-farmers, tenant farmers, oral lessees and sharecroppers — where the lender permits
  • At least one season of cultivation experience for crop loans; agri-allied units usually need 2+ years
  • Clear land records or accepted tenancy proof for the land being cultivated
  • Repayment record and credit history as per the lender; CIBIL is not the only test for small crop loans
  • For equipment finance: a viable farm plan and the machine's hypothecation

Documents

  • PAN, Aadhaar and address proof
  • Land records — pattadar passbook, 1-B, adangal/ROR and encumbrance certificate
  • Tenancy or lease agreement, where the applicant is not the landowner
  • Crop plan — acreage, crop, irrigation source and expected harvest
  • 6–12 months' bank statement of the primary account
  • Tractor or equipment proforma invoice / dealer quotation
  • For agri-allied units: ITR, milk or collection statements, GST returns where applicable
  • Existing loan statement and NOC, if refinancing or switching lenders

Before you apply

Agriculture loan FAQs

What is the difference between a KCC and an agriculture term loan?

A Kisan Credit Card is a revolving credit limit for crop inputs and cultivation expenses, usually renewed each season. An agriculture term loan funds a durable purchase or development — a tractor, irrigation system, land improvement or dairy shed — and is repaid over several years. Many farmers use both together.

Can tenant farmers get a crop loan without owning land?

Several banks and cooperative lenders extend KCC facilities to tenant farmers, oral lessees and sharecroppers, though the documentation and lender appetite vary. A written lease, landowner NOC, crop plan and local verification usually help. We check which partner lender is currently accepting tenancy-based files.

How does the 4% effective crop loan rate work?

Under the interest-subvention scheme, eligible crop loans are priced at 7% p.a., and a 3% prompt-repayment incentive is credited if the loan is repaid on time. That can bring the effective cost to about 4%. The scheme is subject to government rules, loan limits, crop eligibility and the lender's participation — we confirm the current position before you borrow.

Is collateral required for an agriculture loan?

RBI rules allow collateral-free agriculture loans up to ₹2 lakh per borrower, subject to the lender's policy and the scheme. Larger crop loans, tractor loans and land-development facilities are usually secured by the crop, the equipment or the land. We tell you which structure fits your amount.

Can I finance a tractor or used farm equipment?

Yes. New and used tractors, harvesters, rotavators, pump-sets and drip-irrigation systems can usually be financed, with the machine hypothecated to the lender. Used equipment is funded on a valuation, so the model, year, condition and invoice matter. Tenure typically runs 3–7 years.

Can I get a loan for dairy, poultry or fisheries?

Yes, agri-allied activities are eligible with many lenders. Dairy loans may be assessed on milk collection statements, poultry on contract or sales records, and fisheries on pond records and operating history. A short working-capital facility plus a term loan for sheds or equipment is a common structure.

What if the land records are in a family member's name?

Lenders often allow the landowner to join as co-borrower or guarantor while the cultivating family member is the main applicant. An inheritance record, family partition deed or NOC may be needed. We review the papers first so the application is not rejected on a technicality.
