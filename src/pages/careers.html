Why Sobhakrita

Honest incentives

You are paid for approved, healthy files — not for pushing a customer into a loan they cannot repay.

Local, stable hours

What we look for

Experience helps, but these three things matter more.

  • Honesty under targetsYou can say "no" to a bad file, even in the last week of the month.
  • Respect for the customerYou explain, not pressure. You return calls, even with bad news.
  • Follow-throughYou track the pending document, the lender call, the sanction letter — without being reminded.
