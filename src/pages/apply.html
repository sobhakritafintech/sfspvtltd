Before you submit

Application questions

Will applying here affect my CIBIL score?

Not immediately. We start with a soft assessment based on the details you share. A hard enquiry happens only when a specific lender processes a formal application, and we tell you before that step. Applying to five lenders at once is what hurts a score — that is exactly what we help you avoid.

Is there any charge to apply through Sobhakrita?

Sobhakrita does not charge an application fee, consultation fee, file charge, advance fee or security deposit in its own name. Our own service fee is charged only after your loan is disbursed and only with your consent — the amount and scope are disclosed to you in writing before we begin processing any documentation. Lenders may separately charge a processing fee, legal or technical verification fee, stamp duty, CERSAI charges, insurance premium or other statutory amounts; these are payable to the lender or authority and shown in the Key Fact Statement (KFS). See fees & charges.

How long does approval take?

For a clean salaried personal loan file, an indicative decision can come in 48–72 working hours. Home loans, mortgages and industrial loans take longer because they involve legal and technical verification of the property or unit. We give you a realistic timeline after the first call, not a marketing number.

Can I apply if I live outside Chittoor?

Yes. We regularly assist customers across Andhra Pradesh and can coordinate remotely over WhatsApp and email. For property-backed loans and some business loans, a physical verification visit is usually required at the property or business location.

What if I am not sure which loan I need?

Choose "Not sure — help me choose". That is a perfectly good answer. We will ask a few questions about the purpose, amount and repayment capacity, then recommend the product that costs you least over the full tenure — not the one with the highest commission.
