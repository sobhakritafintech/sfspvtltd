Questions, answered

Before you borrow

Is Sobhakrita a bank or an NBFC?

No. Sobhakrita Fintech Solutions Private Limited is a loan facilitator (Direct Selling Agent). We do not lend in our own name. Your loan is sanctioned and disbursed by a partner bank or an RBI-regulated NBFC, and the loan agreement is between you and that lender.

How long does approval take?

For most personal and business loans, an indicative decision arrives within 48-72 working hours once your documents are complete. Home and mortgage loans involve legal and technical verification and typically take 7–15 working days. Timelines are set by the lender, not by us.

What documents will I need?

Usually PAN, Aadhaar (for KYC), recent address proof, and income proof — salary slips and bank statements for salaried applicants, or ITR, GST returns and bank statements for business owners. Secured loans additionally need property documents. We give you an exact checklist before you start.

Will a low CIBIL score stop my application?

Not always. A score of 700+ improves your chances and rate, but some partner NBFCs consider scores from 650 with additional documentation or a co-applicant. We will tell you honestly where you stand before you pay any lender fee.

What does Sobhakrita charge me?

Sobhakrita does not charge any application, consultation, file, advance or security deposit in its own name. We charge a minimal service fee only after disbursal and only with your written consent; it is quoted in writing before we begin processing your documentation and supported by a GST invoice. Lender charges — processing fee, legal or technical verification, stamp duty, CERSAI, insurance premium and other statutory amounts — are set by the lender or authority and disclosed in the sanction terms and Key Fact Statement (KFS). See fees & charges.

How do I pay Sobhakrita's fees safely?

Only to the company's official current account or verified company UPI, and always against a GST invoice. We never ask for cash or a transfer to an individual's personal account. If anyone claiming to represent Sobhakrita does, stop and report it to services@sobhakrita.com immediately.
