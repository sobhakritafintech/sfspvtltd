Buy well, borrow wisely

The cheapest large loan you will ever get — if you structure it right.

A home loan is secured by the property, which is why banks can offer it at a fraction of personal loan rates. But a 20-year commitment deserves more than a quick sanction. The down payment, the tenure, the reset clause and the prepayment terms all matter.

Who can apply

Eligibility and documents

Indicative criteria. Your own contribution and the property's legal clarity matter as much as income.

Eligibility

  • Age 21 to 65 years at sanction (repayment by 70–75)
  • Salaried or self-employed with stable, documented income
  • Minimum net monthly income of ₹25,000 (varies by city and lender)
  • CIBIL score of 700+ preferred
  • 10–25% own contribution, depending on loan amount
  • Clear, marketable title and an approved building plan

Documents

  • PAN, Aadhaar and address proof
  • 3 months' salary slips + 6 months' bank statement, or 2–3 years' ITR for self-employed
  • Sale deed / title deed and mother deed chain
  • Encumbrance certificate and latest property tax receipts
  • Approved building plan and occupancy certificate (where applicable)
  • NOC from the builder/society, and payment receipts
  • Property insurance and, where the lender requires it, loan-protection cover — we help you compare premiums

Before you apply

Home loan FAQs

How much down payment will I need?

Typically 10% for loans up to ₹30 lakh, 20% between ₹30 lakh and ₹75 lakh, and 25% above that, as per prevailing RBI-linked norms adopted by lenders. Some lenders fund up to 90% for eligible profiles. Registration and stamp duty are usually paid separately by you.

Can I transfer an existing home loan?

Yes. A balance transfer can reduce your rate, and a top-up can fund renovation or another goal. We compare the new rate against the transfer cost (processing, legal, stamp duty) so you only move if it genuinely saves money.

Is a co-applicant necessary?

Not always, but a co-applicant — usually a spouse, parent or adult child — can increase the loan amount you qualify for. Co-applicants also become joint owners or guarantors as per the lender's requirement.

Can I prepay without a penalty?

For individual borrowers on floating-rate home loans, RBI rules do not permit foreclosure charges. Fixed-rate loans may carry a prepayment fee as per the lender's policy, so we check this before you choose the product.

How long does disbursal take?

Once the property's legal and technical verification is complete and the agreement is signed, disbursal usually happens within 7–15 working days. Under-construction properties are disbursed in stages as per the builder's demand schedule.
