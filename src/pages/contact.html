Grievance redressal

If something goes wrong, we want to know.

Sobhakrita follows a three-level escalation process. Most concerns are resolved at the first level within seven working days.

Level 1 — Somasekhar Anugunti, Regional Manager

Call or write to somasekhar.anugunti@sobhakrita.com or call +91 88866 33118. Include your name, mobile number and application number. Target resolution: 2 working days.

Level 2 — Shalini, Director & Grievance Officer

If unresolved, write to grievance@sobhakritafintech.com or call +91 88866 33119. Target resolution: 7 days from receipt.

Level 3 — RBI Ombudsman

For complaints about a regulated lender, you may approach the RBI's Integrated Ombudsman Scheme after the lender's own escalation timeline. We will share the lender's details.

Sobhakrita Fintech Solutions Private Limited is a loan facilitator. Complaints regarding a specific loan product, rate or recovery practice may need to be raised with the concerned lender first, as the loan agreement is between you and that lender.
