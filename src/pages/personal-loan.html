The right use of a personal loan

  • Age 21 to 60 years at application
  • Salaried with 6+ months in current job, or self-employed with 2+ years of business

  • CIBIL score of 700+ preferred; 650+ considered by select NBFCs

  • Recent address proof (utility bill, rent agreement or Aadhaar)

  • Passport-size photograph and employment/office ID

Before you apply

Personal loan FAQs

Can I get a personal loan without salary slips?

Yes, if you are self-employed. Lenders typically ask for two years of income tax returns, computation of income and 6–12 months of business bank statements instead. Some NBFCs also accept GST returns or a formal income assessment.

How much can I borrow on my salary?

Most lenders keep total EMIs — including the new loan — within 40–50% of your net monthly income. On a ₹40,000 net salary with no existing EMIs, that usually supports a loan of around ₹8–12 lakh for 60 months, depending on rate and profile. We calculate this precisely before applying.

Will applying affect my CIBIL score?

Each formal application creates a hard enquiry, which can shave a few points. That is why we do a soft assessment and shortlist the right lender first, instead of firing applications at every bank.

Can I prepay or foreclose the loan early?

Yes. Prepayment terms differ between fixed and floating rate products and between banks and NBFCs. We check the exact clause before you sign, and help you request foreclosure when the time comes.

Do you ask for any advance fee?

No. Sobhakrita does not collect any application, consultation, file, advance fee or security deposit in its own name. We are compensated by the lender when your loan is sanctioned. Our minimal service fee applies only after disbursal, with your written consent, and is disclosed in writing before documentation begins.
