Choosing well

Three questions we ask before recommending a loan

The cheapest advertised rate is rarely the whole story. These three questions usually reveal the right answer.

  • What is the money for? A durable asset can justify a longer, secured loan. A short-lived expense should not.
  • What EMI can your worst month handle? We size the loan around the month your income dips, not the month it peaks.
  • What is the true cost? Rate, processing fee, insurance, prepayment terms and total interest — compared as one number.

Common questions

Loan services, explained

Can I apply for more than one loan at a time?

Yes, but every application creates a credit enquiry and too many enquiries in a short period can lower your score. We help you sequence applications so the strongest one is assessed first.

Do you charge for the comparison?

No application or consultation fee. Comparison and the first consultation are free. Sobhakrita is compensated by the lender when a loan is sanctioned, and our minimal service fee applies only after disbursal, with your written consent — quoted in writing before documentation begins. Lender charges such as processing fee, stamp duty, CERSAI and insurance are separate and shown in the Key Fact Statement. See fees & charges.
