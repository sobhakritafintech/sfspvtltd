Payment safety

How to pay us safely.

  • No upfront charge, ever. If anyone asks you to pay an application, consultation, file, advance fee or security deposit in our name before disbursal, it is not us.
  • Our service fee comes after disbursal. It is charged only with your written consent, quoted before documentation begins and supported by a GST invoice.
  • Pay our service fee only to Sobhakrita Fintech Solutions Private Limited through its official current account or verified company UPI.
  • Lender charges go to the lender. Pay processing fee, verification, stamp duty, CERSAI or insurance through the lender's or authority's official channel and keep the receipt.
  • Ask for a written statement of every fee — ours and the lender's — before documentation begins. It should state the amount, the purpose and when it applies.
  • Never pay in cash, by gift card, or to an individual's personal account, wallet or UPI ID — including a processing fee.
  • Never share an OTP to \"release\" a loan. No legitimate lender or facilitator needs one.
  • If something feels off, stop and call +91 88866 33119 before paying anything.
FAQ

Questions about fees.

Does Sobhakrita charge an application or consultation fee?

No. Sobhakrita does not charge an application fee, consultation fee, file charge, advance fee or security deposit in its own name. Our own service fee is minimal, charged only after your loan is disbursed and only with your consent — the amount and scope are given to you in writing before we begin processing any documentation.

How is Sobhakrita compensated?

We charge a minimal service fee after disbursal, with your written consent.

When is your service fee charged?

Only after your loan is disbursed, and only with your written consent. The amount is quoted in writing before we begin processing your documentation and is supported by a GST invoice.

What if my loan is not sanctioned?

No service fee is payable, because no loan has been disbursed. Any lender charges you paid are governed by that lender's own refund or adjustment policy — ask the lender for it in writing. We will help you follow up, but we cannot refund a fee we never received.

What charges does the lender levy?

Lenders may charge a processing fee, legal or technical verification fee, stamp duty, CERSAI charges, insurance premium and other statutory amounts. These are set by the lender or authority, payable to them, and disclosed in the sanction terms and Key Fact Statement (KFS).

Can I pay a processing fee to an individual?

Never. A processing fee or any other lender charge is paid only to the lender or the relevant authority through its official channel, and you should keep the receipt. Never transfer it to an individual's personal account, UPI ID or wallet. If anyone claiming to represent Sobhakrita asks you to, stop and call us on +91 88866 33119.

Do you charge for using the EMI calculator or this website?

No. The website, EMI calculator and educational content are free to use. An EMI shown by the calculator is indicative and excludes lender charges and our service charge.
