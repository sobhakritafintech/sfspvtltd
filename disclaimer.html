1. General information

The information on www.sobhakritafintech.com is provided by Sobhakrita Fintech Solutions Private Limited (“Sobhakrita”, “we”, “us”) for general information and convenience. While we try to keep product details, rates and processes accurate and current, we make no representation or warranty that everything on the site is complete, error-free or up to date at the moment you read it.

Product features, lender policies, interest rates and regulatory requirements change frequently. Always confirm the final terms with the concerned lender and in the lender's sanction letter and Key Fact Statement (KFS) before signing anything.

2. We are not a lender

Sobhakrita Fintech Solutions Private Limited is a loan facilitator and Direct Selling Agent (DSA). We are not a bank, a non-banking financial company (NBFC), a housing finance company, a deposit-taking institution or an entity authorised by the Reserve Bank of India to lend. We do not sanction, disburse or recover loans in our own name, and we do not accept deposits.

All loans described on this website are offered by partner banks and RBI-regulated NBFCs under their own terms, credit policies and loan agreements. The contractual relationship for a loan is between you and the lender, not with Sobhakrita. Any reference to “our” loan products, rates or turnaround times should be read as referring to products we facilitate through those lenders.

Sobhakrita may be appointed as a DSA by individual lenders. A DSA arrangement does not make us the lender's agent for all purposes, and it does not authorise us to bind a lender or approve credit.

3. Rates and figures are indicative

Interest rates, loan amounts, tenures, processing fees, foreclosure charges, penal charges and eligibility criteria shown on this site are indicative ranges. They are not quotes and do not constitute an offer. The actual rate and terms offered to you depend on factors including:

  • your income, employment stability and repayment history;
  • your credit score and existing credit obligations (FOIR);
  • the lender's product, credit policy and internal pricing grid;
  • the loan amount, tenure, collateral and location; and
  • applicable statutory charges and prevailing market conditions.

Annualised percentage rate (APR) examples on product pages include the principal, interest and certain fees for illustration. Your actual APR may differ. Always compare the APR and total cost of borrowing, not only the headline interest rate.

4. No guarantee of approval

Nothing on this website guarantees that you will be approved for a loan, for a particular amount, at a particular rate, or within a stated timeline. Approvals and disbursals are at the sole discretion of the lender and are subject to its KYC, credit, income, legal and technical checks.

Indicative timelines such as “24–48 working hours” refer to typical cases with complete documentation and a clean profile. They are not commitments. Delays can occur due to pending documents, verification, lender processing volumes, property title issues or regulatory checks.

5. Not financial or legal advice

The content on this site is not investment, financial, tax, accounting or legal advice. It does not take into account your complete financial circumstances, goals or risk tolerance. Before taking a loan or making a financial decision, consider your ability to repay and, where appropriate, consult a qualified professional.

Our advisors explain products and processes; they do not provide investment advice or recommend that you borrow beyond your repayment capacity. If a loan does not suit your situation, we may tell you so.

6. EMI calculator results

The EMI calculator uses the standard reducing-balance formula and is provided for estimation only. Results assume:

  • a fixed interest rate for the entire tenure;
  • monthly compounding and repayment on the same date each month;
  • no processing fee, insurance, stamp duty or other charges in the EMI figure; and
  • no part-prepayment, moratorium or rate change during the loan.

Actual EMIs may differ based on the lender's calculation method, disbursal date, day-count convention, floating-rate resets and charges. The amortisation schedule is indicative and should not be used for accounting or tax purposes.

7. Credit score and CIBIL

References to CIBIL, credit scores and credit reports are for general explanation. CIBIL is a brand of TransUnion CIBIL Limited, one of several credit information companies operating in India. We are not a credit bureau and do not generate or correct credit scores ourselves.

A formal loan application may result in a hard enquiry on your credit report, which can affect your score. We aim to minimise unnecessary enquiries, but the decision to apply to a particular lender is ultimately yours.

8. Fees, charges and fraud warning

Sobhakrita does not charge any application, consultation, file, advance fee or security deposit in its own name. We charge a minimal service fee only after disbursal, with your written consent, and disclose the full fee in writing before documentation begins. Lender charges — processing fee, legal or technical verification, stamp duty, CERSAI, insurance premium and other statutory amounts — are set by the lender or the relevant authority, disclosed in the sanction terms and KFS, and paid to them. Never pay a processing fee or any other charge to an individual's personal account, UPI ID or wallet.

If anyone claims to represent Sobhakrita and asks you to pay before loan approval, share an OTP, send original documents by courier, or transfer money to release a loan, treat it as a potential fraud. Stop, do not pay, and verify by calling +91 88866 33119 or writing to services@sobhakrita.com. You may also report cyber fraud to the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal.
