Questions, answered

Before you borrow

Is Sobhakrita a bank or an NBFC?

No. Sobhakrita Fintech Solutions Private Limited is a loan facilitator (Direct Selling Agent). We do not lend in our own name. Your loan is sanctioned and disbursed by a partner bank or an RBI-regulated NBFC, and the loan agreement is between you and that lender.

How long does approval take?

For most personal and business loans, an indicative decision arrives within 24–48 working hours once your documents are complete. Home and mortgage loans involve legal and technical verification and typically take 7–15 working days. Timelines are set by the lender, not by us.

What documents will I need?

Usually PAN, Aadhaar (for KYC), recent address proof, and income proof — salary slips and bank statements for salaried applicants, or ITR, GST returns and bank statements for business owners. Secured loans additionally need property documents. We give you an exact checklist before you start.

Will a low CIBIL score stop my application?

Not always. A score of 700+ improves your chances and rate, but some partner NBFCs consider scores from 650 with additional documentation or a co-applicant. We will tell you honestly where you stand before you pay any lender fee.

What does Sobhakrita charge me?

Sobhakrita charges a minimal service fee only after disbursal and only with your written consent; it is quoted in writing before we begin processing your documentation and supported by a GST invoice. Lender charges — processing fee, legal or technical verification, stamp duty, CERSAI, insurance premium and other statutory amounts — are set by the lender or authority and disclosed in the sanction terms and Key Fact Statement (KFS).

Does Sobhakrita ask for an advance fee?

No. We do not ask for any application fee, consultation fee, file charge, advance fee or security deposit in our own name before disbursal. Our service fee is minimal and applies only after your loan is disbursed, with your written consent, and is disclosed in writing before documentation begins. If anyone claims to represent Sobhakrita and demands money upfront, please report it to services@sobhakrita.com immediately.
