Finance the productive asset

A machine that earns should not be bought with costly money.

For a manufacturing unit, the right loan is usually tied to the asset it funds — a CNC machine, a packaging line, a fabrication shed — with a tenure matching the asset's working life. That keeps the EMI proportional to the income it generates.

Sobhakrita prepares the project note, validates the machinery quotation, maps the repayment to your production and receivable cycle, and identifies lenders comfortable with your sector. Where your unit is eligible, we also evaluate collateral-free guarantee schemes for MSMEs.

Before you apply

MSME loan FAQs

What is a CGTMSE-style guarantee scheme?

It is a credit guarantee mechanism that allows lenders to sanction collateral-free loans to eligible MSMEs, with the guarantee fund absorbing a defined portion of the default risk. Eligibility, sector caps and fees are set by the scheme and the lender.

Can a new manufacturing unit get finance?

Yes, but it is assessed as a project rather than a running business. Lenders will need a detailed project report, promoter experience, machinery quotations and a clear repayment plan. A moratorium during installation is often possible.

How much of the machinery cost is funded?

Typically 75–85% of the invoice value, with the promoter contributing the margin. Used machinery is funded at lower ratios and may require additional collateral.

Can I get working capital along with a term loan?

Yes. A composite facility — term loan for the asset plus a cash-credit limit for operations — is common and often priced better than two separate products. We structure and compare both together.

Do you finance service businesses and traders?

Yes, subject to the lender's sector policy. The industrial/MSME route is most relevant to manufacturing, but allied activities such as fabrication, packaging, cold storage and logistics are also financed.
