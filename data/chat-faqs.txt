Q: What is a CIBIL score?
A: A CIBIL score is a credit history score used by lenders to assess repayment behaviour. In general, a score of 700 or above is considered healthier, but the final minimum score and approval decision depend on the lender and the loan product.

TAGS: cibil, credit score, credit history, score, eligibility

Q: What documents do I need to apply?

Q: What documents are required for a salaried person?
A: Salaried applicants usually need PAN, Aadhaar, address proof, last 3 months salary slips, 6 months bank statements, and employment details. Final requirements vary by lender.
